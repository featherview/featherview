# Roadmap

Where Featherview is going. Ideas and wishes are welcome in
[Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/featherview/featherview/discussions/categories/ideas).

## Now: beta, on the way to 1.0

0.10 has everything planned for 1.0: the viewer, thumbnail grid, slideshow, compare, batch
conversion, the extra formats with Finder previews, and 30 languages.

- Fix what beta testers find. Please tell us in
  [Beta feedback](https://github.com/featherview/featherview/discussions/categories/beta-feedback).
- Signed and notarized releases, so the first launch needs no "Open Anyway", once
  [sponsorships](https://github.com/sponsors/mynetx) cover Apple's $99/year developer membership.

## Done, in the next release

- Thumbnail sizes that snap to whole columns filling the window, as in Photos
- Right-click on thumbnails: view, copy, move, Show in Finder, batch convert or trash the selection
- Drag thumbnails out of the window into Finder, Mail or any app
- Automatic updates: Featherview downloads, checks and installs a new version, then relaunches

## After 1.0

- Photos library: browse albums from Apple's Photos with the arrow keys, drag pictures in from
  Photos, and batch convert from it or into it, without exporting first
- Richer info panel: a histogram, an exposure card, foldable groups, location with Open in Maps,
  and every metadata tag on request

## Not planned

Painting and editing beyond the batch steps, printing, scanning, OCR, plugins, slideshow effects,
video, and versions for Windows or Linux.
