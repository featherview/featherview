# Roadmap

Where Featherview is going. Ideas and wishes are welcome in
[Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/featherview/featherview/discussions/categories/ideas).

## Now: beta, on the way to 1.0

0.13 has everything planned for 1.0: the viewer, thumbnail grid, slideshow, compare, batch
conversion, the extra formats with Finder previews, the info panel with histogram and place names,
30 languages and automatic updates. New in 0.13:

- Huge pictures: Hubble's 4.6 GB, 1.5-gigapixel Andromeda file is on screen about two seconds after
  the double-click, app launch included; huge TIFFs open several times faster, and zooming in reads
  only the part on screen
- Photoshop layers: files saved without "Maximize Compatibility" show the picture Photoshop shows,
  drawn from their layers with every blend mode, groups, masks, fill and adjustment layers
- Photos library: browse albums from Apple's Photos with the arrow keys, drag pictures in from
  Photos, and batch convert from it or into it, without exporting first

0.13.1 shows a sharp preview of a Photos picture that is only in iCloud while it downloads.

Next:

- Fix what beta testers find. Please tell us in
  [Beta feedback](https://github.com/featherview/featherview/discussions/categories/beta-feedback).
- Signed and notarized releases, so the first launch needs no extra step, once
  [sponsorships](https://github.com/sponsors/mynetx) cover Apple's $99/year developer membership.

## Planned

- **1.0: polish and hardening.** No new features until 1.0: fixing what early users report, a
  simplify pass over the older code, checks for stability, memory and speed on more real-world
  files, translations reviewed by native speakers, and a
  notarized download once sponsors cover Apple's fee.
- **After 1.0: your requests.** Missing something? Ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/featherview/featherview/discussions); the most-wanted ideas go here.

## Not planned

Painting and editing beyond the batch steps, printing, scanning, OCR, plugins, slideshow effects,
video, and versions for Windows or Linux.
