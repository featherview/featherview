Featherview: opening it the first time
======================================

Featherview isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first
time you open it. One line in Terminal lets it open, on any Mac account,
administrator or not:

1. Drag Featherview to Applications. On a standard account, macOS asks
   for an administrator's name and password here.

2. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line
   and press Return:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Featherview.app

3. Open Featherview. From now on it opens like any other app.

The line removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark from Featherview
only. Nothing else on your Mac changes.

Administrator? Instead of step 2 you can open Featherview once, then go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On standard
accounts Open Anyway often fails on recent macOS versions, so use the
Terminal line there.


Why the extra step
------------------

Featherview is made in our spare time and given away for free. For macOS
to open it straight away, Apple has to notarize it, and that costs
99 EUR / 99 USD every year. If Featherview turns out useful to you, a
small contribution towards that fee would mean a lot:

   https://github.com/sponsors/mynetx

Thank you for trying it either way.

Featherview is open source: https://github.com/featherview/featherview
